Privacy Policy

Ventema collects nothing. This page explains what that means in practice, and what the two Apple services it uses do with your data.

Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version

Ventema has no server, no account system and no analytics. Everything you enter — your groves, their locations, what they yielded, the plan and the aftercare list — is stored on your device and, unless you turn iCloud off, mirrored to your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot read any of it. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to a third party, because nothing is sent anywhere at all.

Who this policy is from

Ventema is made by an independent developer, who is the data controller for the limited purposes described here. Questions about this policy go to support@ventema.app.

What the app stores

All of the following stays on your device, and is written by you:

  • Groves: name, notes, number of trees, priority, and the coordinates you pin.
  • Harvest records: litres of oil, kilos of olives, working days, picker counts, dates and notes, kept per season.
  • The harvest plan: which grove you intend to pick on which day.
  • Aftercare jobs: what needs doing, when it is due, and whether it is done.
  • App settings, such as the season you are looking at.

The app asks for no name, no email address, no phone number and no date of birth. It has no sign-up screen, because it has nothing to sign you up to.

iCloud syncing

If you are signed into iCloud and have iCloud Drive on, Ventema mirrors its database to the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account, so your other devices — and the other devices in your household signed into that same account — see the same farm.

A private CloudKit database is readable only by the account that owns it. The developer has no access to it, cannot list it, and receives no copy or summary of what is in it. Apple processes that data as your provider under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Turning iCloud off for Ventema keeps the app entirely on the device; it works exactly the same, minus the syncing.

Location

Ventema asks for location access only while you are using the app, and only so that the map can show where you are and put a pin where you stand. Your position is used on the device in the moment and is not recorded, not tracked in the background, and not transmitted to the developer or anyone else.

The coordinates that are stored are the ones you deliberately attach to a grove. They are your data, treated exactly like the rest of it. You can refuse location access entirely and still use every part of the app by placing pins by hand.

Purchases

The app contains an optional tip jar. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; the app is told only whether a transaction succeeded. No payment details, card numbers or billing information ever reach the app or the developer. Apple's handling of a purchase is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

The developer does not receive your name or email address with a tip, and tips unlock nothing — there is no entitlement to track.

What Ventema does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app is built on Apple's frameworks alone.
  • No crash-reporting service. Apple may share aggregate crash data with the developer if you have opted into sharing analytics with Apple, under Apple's own controls.
  • No back end, no API, and no account database.

Children

Ventema is a tool for farm work and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Data-protection law — the GDPR included — gives you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability over personal data held about you. Because the developer holds none, these rights are exercised directly on your device:

  • Access and correction: everything is visible and editable in the app.
  • Deletion: delete a grove, or delete the app itself. To remove the synced copy, delete Ventema from every device on the account, then turn it off under Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud.
  • Portability: your data lives in your own iCloud account and moves with it.

There is no request to make of the developer, and no dataset to delete on your behalf — but if something here is unclear, ask.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Ventema ever does something this page does not describe, this page changes before that version ships, and the date at the top changes with it. A change that would mean collecting anything personal would be announced in the app itself and would ask for your agreement first.
