Privacy Policy

Ventema collects nothing. Here is what it stores, and where.

Last updated 13 September 2026

What the app stores

Your groves, harvest records, plan, aftercare jobs and settings — all entered by you, and kept on your device. The app asks for no name, email address or phone number.

iCloud syncing

If iCloud is on, that data is mirrored to the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account, so your devices see the same farm. Only your account can read it; the developer cannot, and receives no copy. Apple processes it under Apple's Privacy Policy. Turn iCloud off for Ventema and everything stays on the device.

Location

Used only while you are using the app, to show where you are and pin a grove. It is not recorded, tracked in the background or sent anywhere; the only coordinates stored are the ones you attach to a grove. You can refuse it and place pins by hand.

Tips

The optional tip jar runs through Apple's App Store. The app learns only whether a purchase succeeded — no payment details, name or email reach the app or the developer.

What Ventema does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs — only Apple's frameworks.
  • No crash-reporting service. Apple may share aggregate crash data with the developer if you have opted into sharing analytics with Apple.
  • No back end, API or account database.

Your rights

Data-protection law, the GDPR included, gives you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability. Because the developer holds no data about you, you exercise them in the app: everything is visible and editable there, and your data moves with your iCloud account. To remove the synced copy, delete Ventema from every device on the account, then turn it off under Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud.

Children, changes and contact

Ventema is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone. If a future version ever does something this page does not describe, this page changes first, along with the date above, and anything that would mean collecting personal data will ask for your agreement first.
