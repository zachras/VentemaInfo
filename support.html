Common questions

My groves are not reaching the other phone

Both devices must be signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on, and Ventema must be allowed to use it — check Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Saved to iCloud, and find Ventema in the list. Settings inside the app shows what it can see of your account; if it reports a problem there, that is the thing to fix first. Syncing is not instant: it goes when the device has a connection, and a change made in a grove with no signal arrives once you are back in range.

Can my family use it without sharing my Apple Account?

Not yet. Ventema syncs through the private iCloud database of one account, which is what lets it work with no sign-up and no servers. Households that share the farm today sign every device into the same Apple Account. Sharing a farm between separate accounts is the most requested change and is being looked at.

The map will not show where I am

Ventema asks for your location only to centre the map and to place a pin where you are standing. If you said no at the time, turn it back on in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services ▸ Ventema, and choose While Using the App. You can decline it entirely and still use everything — you place pins by hand instead.

Where did last year's numbers go?

Nowhere. Harvest data is kept per season, and a season is named for the autumn it opens in — 2025–26 covers the picking from autumn 2025 into 2026. Change the season in Settings and the map, the plan and the grove list all move to that year together. Change it back and this winter returns exactly as it was.

What counts as a working day?

Whatever you decide it is, and halves are allowed — an afternoon in the grove is 0.5. The number is there so a grove can be judged against what it costs in labour, so being consistent matters more than being precise.

Can I try it before I put my own farm in?

Yes. Settings has a sample farm you can load: a handful of groves with a season's worth of picking already in them, so you can see how the plan, the totals and the aftercare list behave before entering anything real.

I deleted a grove by mistake

Deleting a grove removes its harvest records and its aftercare jobs with it, and the app cannot bring it back. If the deletion has not yet reached your other device, force-quitting Ventema there before it syncs sometimes leaves a copy — but do not rely on it. The safe course is to keep a device in Airplane Mode and write for help before doing anything else.

I paid a tip and nothing happened

That is the intended behaviour — tips unlock nothing. Every feature is available to everyone, paid or not, and always will be. If you were charged twice or a purchase failed halfway, Apple handles the refund: use reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete everything?

Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. To remove the synced copy as well, delete it from every device signed into the account and then turn Ventema off under Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored and where.

Reporting a bug

Four things make a report almost always fixable:

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • The steps that got you there, in order.
  • Your iOS version and device, from Settings ▸ General ▸ About.
  • Ventema's version number, at the bottom of the app's Settings tab.

Send them to support@ventema.app. Please do not include anything you would not want in an ordinary email; a screenshot with the grove names blurred is perfectly useful.

Suggesting something

Ventema is built from how one family picks olives, which means it is certainly missing something about how yours does. Descriptions of the problem are more useful than descriptions of the feature — what you are trying to keep track of, and what you do today instead. Those are read properly, and several are already in the app.
